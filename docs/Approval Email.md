My proposed project is Inside, an interior design service platform that connects clients with interior designers through a structured workflow. In this system, users can either browse and select designers based on their profiles, portfolios, and styles, or create a project request describing their requirements such as room type, budget, and preferences. Designers can view these requests and respond with proposals including pricing and timelines. Once a designer is selected, both client and designer interact within the platform to complete the design process.

The system includes key features such as messaging for communication, design submission and updates, client feedback and revisions, milestone-based payments with payouts for designers, designer availability management, project status tracking, wishlist functionality, notifications, and a review and rating system.
