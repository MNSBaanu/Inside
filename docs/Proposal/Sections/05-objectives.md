## Objectives

* Conduct a critical review of the interior design service domain and identify limitations in existing digital platforms used for designer discovery, project collaboration, and payment management.
* Critically study technologies suitable for developing a modern web and mobile platform, including frontend, backend, database, cloud, and payment integration technologies.
* Design and develop an Interior Design Service Platform that supports designer discovery, project collaboration, communication, trust and safety mechanisms, and secure payment management through web and mobile applications.
* Evaluate the proposed system against functional and non-functional requirements through testing, user feedback, and performance assessment.
* Prepare comprehensive project documentation, including technical documentation, user documentation, and the final dissertation report.
