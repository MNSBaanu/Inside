# Introduction

The interior design sector has expanded as homeowners and businesses invest in tailored living and working environments, and much of how clients find practitioners and how designers present their work now happens online. Yet **clients** and **interior designers** still often manage commissions through disconnected steps—finding a professional, explaining what is wanted, handling revisions, and paying by stage frequently use separate tools or informal arrangements, with no shared record of what was agreed or approved.

That fragmentation causes real harm. When briefing relies on text alone, style and spatial intent are easily lost, which drives rework and friction on both sides. Interior jobs also involve meaningful financial commitment, so uncertainty about progress or payment undermines confidence long before a space is finished. Keeping activity in one dependable process can keep feedback in context, make approvals visible, and release funds only after accepted work—outcomes that are difficult when messages, alerts, and payments sit on different channels.

This project will develop a **web and mobile platform** that **connects clients and interior designers in one environment** from inspiration to completion, using **Next.js** for the web interface, a **React Native** mobile application for access on phones and tablets, **Node.js and NestJS** for application logic, **PostgreSQL** with **Prisma** for data persistence, **Cloudinary** for media storage, and **Stripe** for secure online payments tied to client approval. The **web application** supports the full feature set: secure registration and login, portfolio discovery with search and filters, moodboards and editable structured briefs, fit scoring, open and direct hiring with proposal comparison, a stage-gated project workspace, real-time messaging, in-app and email notifications, milestone payments with a transparent ledger, reviews and ratings, trust and safety controls (designer verification, reporting, blocking), and an **administrator** interface for moderation and platform oversight. The **mobile app** supports push notifications, messaging, project progress, stage approval, milestone payment confirmation, and role switching. Clients browse completed work, build visual briefs, save favourite designers, contact designers or post open requests, compare proposals, and confirm progress before payment; designers publish portfolios, respond with scoped stage-based offers, upload deliverables, and communicate on both channels. In summary, the solution is a visual-first, workflow-led interior design service platform intended to unite discovery, structured collaboration, trust, administration, and milestone payment on one system.
