# Aim

The aim of this project is to **design, develop, and evaluate** an Interior Design Service Platform that addresses fragmented designer discovery, project communication, and payment management by providing a centralized environment for clients, interior designers, and administrators through a **web application** and a **mobile application**, with secure user management and role-based access, visual-led discovery and search, moodboards and structured briefs, designer fit scoring, open and direct hiring with proposals, stage-gated collaboration with timelines and activity history, real-time messaging and notifications, milestone payments with a payment ledger, reviews and ratings, trust and safety controls, and administrative moderation and analytics, using **Next.js**, **React Native**, **Node.js / NestJS**, **PostgreSQL**, **Prisma**, **Cloudinary**, cloud hosting, **GitHub**, and **Stripe** payment integration.
