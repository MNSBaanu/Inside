**[Introduction]{.underline}**

This must give a **brief description about the problem area**, **[the
problem]{.mark}** that you address, the importance of the problem,
**[technology]{.mark}** to solve the problem and the nature of the
**[solution]{.mark}** you propose.

(Ideally 2 -- 3 paragraphs)

Example:

For more than 15 years, AHC Tours Pvt Limited has been one of the most
established and trusted public transportation providers in Sri Lanka,
operating a fleet of buses that connect major cities, towns, and rural
communities. However, with increasing passenger demand and the rise of
digital technology, the company's traditional manual booking and
ticketing process has become slow, inefficient, and outdated. Customers
are required to visit physical ticket counters, wait in long queues, and
rely on paper-based tickets, while staff must manually handle seat
allocation, payments, and verification. These issues result in errors,
delays, and poor travel experience for both passengers and employees.

To address these challenges and align with modern transportation
standards, this project proposes the development of a mobile and
web-based digital reservation system specifically designed for AHC
Tours. The solution includes a Flutter-based Passenger Mobile App for
booking seats, viewing real-time availability, making secure online
payments, and receiving QR-coded e-tickets.

Example:

AHC Tours has been serving passengers across Sri Lanka for many years,
but their reservation process is still fully manual. Passengers must
visit ticket counters in person, stand in long queues, and rely on
paper-based systems that often lead to mistakes and delays.

With technology rapidly evolving, most travelers today expect quick,
digital, and mobile-friendly booking options that allow them to plan
their trips easily (Alavi, 2023). The lack of an online or mobile-based
reservation system makes it difficult for AHC Tours to meet these new
expectations and stay competitive in the modern transport industry.

Because of these challenges, there is a strong need for AHC Tours to
move towards a more efficient, automated, and user-friendly solution. A
digital reservation system supported by mobile apps can give passengers
the convenience to check bus schedules, view seat availability, make
secure payments, and receive QR tickets instantly (Wandile, et al.,
2024).

For conductors, a mobile QR-scanning app can speed up boarding, prevent
ticket fraud, and reduce manual verification work (Reddy, 2018). An
admin dashboard can also help the company manage routes, schedules,
bookings, and revenue more accurately. This project is motivated by the
need to modernize AHC Tours' operations, reduce manual workload,
increase transparency, and deliver smoother and more reliable travel
experience for both passengers and staff (Erangi, 2023).

**[Problem in Brief]{.underline}**

What is the specific problem that you hope to solve and why is it worth
solving?

2 -- 3 paragraphs max

Example:

The main challenge faced by AHC Tours is the continued dependence on a
fully manual reservation and ticketing process, which is inefficient and
outdated in today's digital era. Since there is not online or
mobile-based booking system, passengers must visit ticket counters
physically to reserve seats, resulting in long queues, overcrowding, and
inconvenience, especially during peak travel times.

Manual seat allocation increases the chances of errors such as double
bookings, unclear seat availability, and delays in communication between
staff members. Ticket verification at boarding is also done using paper
tickets, which can be damaged, lost, or easily duplicated, leading to
slow boarding, security issues, and frequent operational delays. Without
a real-time system, staff cannot track seat occupancy or route
performance effectively, making it difficult to make timely and informed
decisions.

**[Proposed Solution]{.underline}**

Explain **what you are going to do**, what type of **architecture** you
will use, maybe use some high level diagrams to model your system and
explain, etc.

Example:

The AHC Tours Online Bus Seat Reservation System digitizes the complete
bus ticketing workflow by integrating eight core functional areas into a
centralized platform. The system enables passengers to perform Online
Booking through a responsive web application, select seats using an
Interactive Seat Selection interface with real-time availability, and
complete transactions via secure Payment Processing through the PayHere
gateway. Upon successful payment, passengers receive QR-Coded Digital
Tickets via email and WhatsApp, which conductors verify instantly using
a Ticket Verification mobile app that scans and validates bookings in
real-time.

The platform supports Admin Management through a comprehensive dashboard
for managing buses, routes, schedules, and bookings, provides Real-time
Availability updates across all user sessions to prevent double
bookings, and offers Multi-language Support in English, Sinhala, and
Tamil to ensure accessibility for Sri Lanka\'s diverse population. This
integrated solution eliminates manual inefficiencies, reduces errors,
accelerates boarding processes, and delivers a modern, professional
transportation service experience for both staff and passengers.
