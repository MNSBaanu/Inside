The main problem addressed by this project is the lack of a dedicated platform that supports the complete interaction process between clients and interior designers. Clients often rely on multiple platforms and communication channels to discover design inspiration, find suitable designers, discuss requirements, manage project progress, and handle payments. This fragmented approach makes it difficult to maintain clear communication, track project activities, and ensure that both parties have a shared understanding of project expectations.

Interior design projects are highly visual and frequently involve multiple revisions before reaching a final outcome. Clients may find it difficult to communicate their preferences using text-based descriptions alone, while designers may not receive sufficient information to accurately understand client expectations. In addition, project approvals and payment processes are often handled through separate tools, creating inefficiencies and reducing transparency throughout the project lifecycle. The absence of mechanisms that promote trust, accountability, and effective issue resolution can further reduce user confidence when engaging with unfamiliar service providers.

This problem is worth solving because ineffective communication, unclear requirements, and disconnected workflows can lead to project delays, repeated revisions, and reduced satisfaction for both clients and designers. A dedicated platform that combines designer discovery, project interaction, communication, and milestone-based payment management within a single environment can improve transparency, support better collaboration, and provide a more structured approach to managing interior design projects (IBM, 2024; Stripe, 2024).
