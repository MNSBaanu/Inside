# Problem in Brief

The specific problem addressed by this project is the **absence of a unified, interior-design-specific platform** that connects visual discovery, designer selection, structured collaboration, and milestone-based payments. Today, clients typically browse inspiration on one website, message designers on another, and arrange payments offline. This fragmented process creates three recurring issues.

**First**, clients express requirements through text descriptions that poorly capture style intent, leading to mismatched proposals and costly revision cycles. **Second**, hiring decisions are often based on generic profile ratings rather than demonstrated compatibility with the client’s preferred room types, styles, and budget. **Third**, project progress and payments lack transparency — there is no single system of record for stage deliverables, approvals, and fund release.

These problems are worth solving because interior design projects involve significant financial commitment and multiple revision rounds. A platform that lets clients build visual briefs (moodboards), compare structured proposals, and approve work at defined stages before releasing payment benefits both clients (control and clarity) and designers (fair milestones and reduced scope disputes).

Inside addresses this by providing one environment where discovery, hiring, collaboration, and payment are integrated around a workflow that mirrors real interior design practice: proposal, concept, design, revision, and completion — with milestone payments tied to client approval at key stages.
