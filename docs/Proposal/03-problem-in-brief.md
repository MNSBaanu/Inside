# Problem in Brief

The specific problem is the absence of one interior-design-focused service that carries a commission from hiring a designer through collaboration to payment. Clients use separate channels to find ideas, contact a practitioner, and pay, with no shared record of what was agreed or accepted at each point. Designers often receive vague written instructions, so responses miss the client’s intent and revision cycles repeat. Hiring may rely on limited evidence of relevant past work, and delivery, acceptance, and payment are not held on the same job record.

This is worth solving because interior jobs involve significant cost and many rounds of change; early misunderstanding and payment uncertainty create avoidable loss for both sides (Stripe, 2024). A dedicated web platform can apply established multi-tier design to a field still served by fragmented tools (IBM, 2024). The project will therefore build an integrated environment where clients and interior designers discover one another, manage an active commission together, and complete payment only after accepted progress.
