# Problem in Brief

The specific problem this project targets is the lack of a unified, interior-design-specific service that carries a commission from finding a designer through ongoing collaboration to payment. Clients often gather inspiration on one site, contact a practitioner on another, and settle fees outside any shared record of what was agreed or approved at each stage. Designers receive instructions that do not reliably express style or spatial intent, which leads to responses that miss the mark and to repeated revision cycles. Hiring choices are often made with limited evidence of relevant completed work, and there is no single source of truth linking what was delivered, what was accepted, and what was paid on the same job.

The problem is worth solving because interior commissions involve substantial spend and several rounds of feedback. Poor alignment at the outset tends to produce costly rework later, and unclear or disputed payment in creative and professional services undermines trust between customer and supplier (Stripe, 2024). A purpose-built web platform that unifies the client–designer journey can apply proven multi-tier application design to a domain where fragmented tools currently fall short (IBM, 2024).

The proposed system will address this by supporting designer discovery, structured hiring, a shared project area for staged delivery and feedback, and secure payments released only after the client accepts progress—bringing the full client–designer journey into one web-based environment built with **Next.js**, **ASP.NET Core 8**, **AWS**, and **Stripe**.
