# Introduction

The interior design sector has expanded as homeowners and businesses invest in tailored living and working environments, and much of how clients find practitioners and how designers present their work now happens online. Yet **clients** and **interior designers** still often manage commissions through disconnected steps—finding a professional, explaining what is wanted, handling revisions, and paying by stage frequently use separate tools or informal arrangements, with no shared record of what was agreed or approved.

That fragmentation causes real harm. When briefing relies on text alone, style and spatial intent are easily lost, which drives rework and friction on both sides. Interior jobs also involve meaningful financial commitment, so uncertainty about progress or payment undermines confidence long before a space is finished. Keeping activity in one dependable process can keep feedback in context, make approvals visible, and release funds only after accepted work—outcomes that are difficult when messages, alerts, and payments sit on different channels.

This project will develop a **web and mobile platform** that **connects clients and interior designers in one environment** from inspiration to completion, using **Next.js** for the web interface, a **React Native (Expo)** mobile application for access on phones and tablets, **ASP.NET Core 8** for application logic, a relational database on **AWS**, and **Stripe** for secure online payments tied to client approval. The **web application** will provide the fuller browsing and project-management experience on larger screens; the **mobile app** will let clients and designers check progress, respond to messages, and approve work from anywhere. Clients will browse completed work, express preferences visually, compare responses, and confirm progress before payment; designers will publish their work, take on commissions, deliver updates by stage, and communicate through the same system on both channels. In summary, the solution is a dedicated interior design service platform—visual-first and workflow-led—intended to unite discovery, collaboration, and payment on one system throughout each project.
