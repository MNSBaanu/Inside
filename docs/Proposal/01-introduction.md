# Introduction

The interior design industry increasingly depends on digital platforms for inspiration, designer discovery, and project coordination. However, most existing solutions address only part of this journey. Portfolio platforms such as Behance and Dribbble excel at showcasing creative work but provide no structured path to hire a designer, manage revisions, or process payments. General freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr support hiring and transactions but treat interior design like any other service category, relying heavily on text-based profiles rather than visual design compatibility. Dedicated online interior design services such as Havenly and Decorilla offer end-to-end design packages but operate as closed matching systems where the client cannot freely browse designers based on demonstrated completed work.

As a result, clients often use multiple disconnected tools: one for inspiration, another for communication, and informal channels for payment. This fragmentation leads to unclear requirements, repeated revisions, and reduced transparency during project delivery.

This project proposes **Inside**, an interior design service platform that unifies designer discovery, visual preference expression, structured project workflow, and milestone-based payments within a single web-based environment. Clients browse a portfolio-first discovery feed of real completed room projects, save favourites to personal moodboards that act as visual briefs, and hire designers through project requests and proposals or direct engagement. Designers publish case studies, respond with scoped proposals, and collaborate with clients through a stage-based project workspace. The system is developed using modern web technologies including **Next.js**, **Node.js with Express**, **MongoDB Atlas**, **AWS cloud services**, and **Stripe** for secure milestone payments.

The proposed solution is a full-stack, role-based platform with dual-role accounts (client and designer on one login), designed specifically for how interior design projects progress from concept to completion.
