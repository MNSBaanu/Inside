## Background and Motivation

The interior design industry increasingly relies on digital platforms to connect clients with professional designers. As more people seek design services online, the process of discovering designers, communicating requirements, managing projects, and handling payments has become more technology-driven. However, many existing platforms focus only on specific aspects of this process, such as showcasing portfolios or facilitating hiring, resulting in a fragmented user experience.

Interior design projects are highly visual and depend heavily on effective communication between clients and designers. Clients often find it difficult to express their design preferences through text alone, while designers require clear information about styles, budgets, and project expectations before providing suitable solutions. Research indicates that clear communication and structured collaboration play a significant role in improving project outcomes and reducing misunderstandings in creative service environments (IBM, 2024). In addition, secure and transparent payment handling is essential for building trust between service providers and clients (Stripe, 2024).

This problem is worth solving because ineffective communication, unclear project expectations, and disconnected workflows can lead to delays, repeated revisions, and reduced satisfaction for both clients and designers. A platform that combines designer discovery, project interaction, and payment management within a single environment can improve transparency, streamline communication, and provide a more organized experience throughout the design process.

The motivation for undertaking this project comes from the opportunity to apply software engineering principles to a real-world business domain while addressing practical challenges faced by both clients and interior designers. My experience in web application development, database design, software engineering, and API integration provides a strong foundation for undertaking this project. The project also provides an opportunity to apply modern technologies such as cloud services, secure authentication mechanisms, and online payment integration within a real-world business environment.
