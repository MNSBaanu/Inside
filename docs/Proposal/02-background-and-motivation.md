# Background and Motivation

The problem outlined in the introduction sits in a growing interior design market where clients and practitioners meet online more often (IBM, 2024). The work is visual and preference-led, yet intent is hard to express in writing and practitioners need clearer room, budget, and scope signals before accepting a job. Expectations include transparent fees, visible progress, and proof through finished work; few digital offerings support the full relationship from first contact to final payment as one continuous service (IBM, 2024).

That gap is worth addressing because weak alignment leads to costly rework, and disputed or unclear payment undermines trust in creative services (Stripe, 2024). Linking communication, sign-off, and settlement on one job record reduces risk on high-spend, multi-round commissions. The topic also suits a Final Year study—system analysis, cloud implementation, user access control, payment integration, and evaluation in a real domain (IBM, 2024).

I am motivated to deliver a substantial degree artefact for clients and interior designers, and to test whether software shaped for this field serves users better than general hiring sites. My foundation includes web development, relational databases, and API-based work from the degree programme; cloud deployment and payment integration will follow the project plan, with supervisor support, development tools, and volunteer testing before submission.
