## Background and Motivation

The interior design industry increasingly relies on digital platforms to connect clients with professional designers. As demand for online design services grows, activities such as discovering designers, communicating requirements, managing projects, and handling payments have become more technology-driven. However, many existing platforms focus only on specific parts of this process, creating a fragmented experience for both clients and designers.

A review of existing services highlights this gap. Platforms such as Havenly and Decorilla provide online interior design services, while Houzz focuses on inspiration and professional discovery. General freelance marketplaces such as Fiverr and Upwork support hiring and project transactions, and portfolio platforms such as Behance enable designers to showcase their work. Despite these offerings, there is limited support for a single platform that combines portfolio-first designer discovery, structured project collaboration, and milestone-based payment management within one integrated environment.

This problem is worth addressing because interior design projects are highly visual and depend on effective communication between clients and designers. Clients often struggle to communicate their preferences clearly, while designers require accurate information regarding styles, budgets, and project expectations before proposing solutions. Research suggests that structured communication and collaboration improve project outcomes and reduce misunderstandings in creative service environments (IBM, 2024). In addition, secure and transparent payment handling is essential for building trust between clients and service providers (Stripe, 2024).

The motivation for undertaking this project stems from the opportunity to apply software engineering principles to a real-world business domain while addressing practical challenges faced by both clients and interior designers. My background in web application development, database design, software engineering, and API integration provides a strong foundation for undertaking this project. The project also offers an opportunity to apply modern technologies, cloud services, secure authentication mechanisms, and online payment integration while developing a practical solution for the interior design industry.
