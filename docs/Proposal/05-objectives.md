## Objectives

* Conduct a critical review of the interior design service domain and identify limitations in existing digital platforms (e.g. Havenly, Decorilla, Houzz, Fiverr, Behance) used for designer discovery, project interaction, and payment management.

* Critically study technologies suitable for developing a modern web and mobile platform, including Next.js, React Native, Node.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Cloudinary, cloud hosting, GitHub, and Stripe payment integration.

* Design and develop an Interior Design Service Platform that enables clients, designers, and administrators to interact through portfolio-led discovery, moodboards, structured briefs, fit scoring, project requests, proposal comparison, stage-gated collaboration, secure milestone payments, trust and safety controls, and administrative oversight on **web and mobile**.

* Evaluate the proposed system against functional and non-functional requirements through testing and user feedback, including brief clarity, fit score usefulness, and stage-gated workflow effectiveness.

* Prepare comprehensive project documentation, including technical documentation, user documentation, and the final dissertation report.
