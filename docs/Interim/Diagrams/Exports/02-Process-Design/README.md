# Process Design

## 02-activity-hiring.png

Traces how a client moves from finding a suitable designer to a confirmed working relationship. It emphasises decision points, approvals, and the hand-offs that turn interest into an active project.

## 03-activity-workspace.png

Shows how delivery work progresses once a project is active: shared workspace actions, stage-based progress, and feedback loops that keep both parties aligned until completion.

## 04-activity-discover-brief.png

Explains how preference signals (discovery, moodboards, briefs) are turned into a shortlist of designers. It supports the matching idea of the product rather than a purely manual search.

## 05-dfd-context.png

Places the system in its environment: external actors and the main information exchanged with Inside. Useful for explaining system scope and external interfaces at a high level.

## 06-dfd-level1.png

Breaks the platform into major internal processes and data stores. It shows how request, collaboration, delivery, and payment-related information move between subsystems without prescribing UI layout.

## 07-sequence-payment.png

Focuses on the timed collaboration between the application, payment provider, and persistence when a client pays. It clarifies success and failure paths that affect trust and project continuation.

## 08-sequence-accept-proposal.png

Details the moment a proposal becomes a live project: validation, state changes, and notifications that synchronise client and designer views of the agreement.

## 09-sequence-stage-approval.png

Covers review of a delivered stage: submission, approval or rejection, and the follow-on effects on workflow status. It underpins staged delivery and controlled release of work.
